The small questions.

five quick answers
How often do you send it, actually?
Most Sundays since January 2024. I have missed three weeks in two years — once for a death, once for a flu, once because I couldn't get the ending right and refused to send a piece I didn't believe in. I am proudest of the third one.
Is there a paid tier?
No. There will probably never be a paid tier. If there ever is, the free essays will keep being free. The voice doesn't change at the paywall, and I am not interested in the kind of writing that would change at one.
What do you do with my email address?
I put it in a quiet self-hosted list, send you the essay, and never give it to anyone else. No analytics pixels. No referral programs. No third-party anything. Unsubscribe is one click and works the first time.
Will you read my reply?
Yes. Every one. Usually within a week. Sometimes within a month, if it's a hard one. The interesting replies, with permission, become the next essay. I have written four essays this year that started as a reader's reply.
Can I forward an essay to someone?
Please do. The handwritten line at the bottom of every essay — “if you found something here, tell someone” — is not decorative. Forwarding is how this place grows. Tell someone.

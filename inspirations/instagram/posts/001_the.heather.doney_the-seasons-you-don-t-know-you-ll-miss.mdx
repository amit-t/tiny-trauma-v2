# the seasons you don't know you'll miss

## Friction
The ordinary evening you'd trade nothing to skip is the one you'll later want back, and you can't tell which it is while you're in it.

## Seed line
The loud, beautiful, ordinary moments she didn't realize she would someday miss. You only get to call them ordinary in hindsight.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On how the present is unrankable from the inside, and grief is just the bill for not having ranked it.

## Raw caption
Motherhood has been the most beautiful stretching and strengthening of my heart. One minute you're rocking babies to sleep, tying shoes, packing lunches, and kissing scraped knees and somehow those same little hands are walking across graduation stages, holding wedding bouquets, and building lives of their own.

Elder Jeffrey R. Holland said, there is something sacred about a mother's love because it reflects the love of Christ so naturally….patient, sacrificing, nurturing and forgiving. I have felt pieces of the Savior's heart more clearly through loving my children.

I've loved every season. The exhausting ones.
The loud, beautiful, ordinary moments I didn't realize I would someday miss.

Motherhood has softened me, strengthened me, humbled me, and drawn me closer to Him. And through every changing season, I'm endlessly grateful I get to be their mom.

To my 4 incredible children, I love you so much! Thank you for letting me be your mother and for everything you've taught me.

# raising someone to not need you

## Friction
The whole job is to make yourself unnecessary, and the better you do it the more it looks like you did nothing.

## Seed line
Staying quiet when every part of me wants to step in and fix it. The hardest competence is the one that shows up as restraint.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the kind of love measured by what you don't do, and how that's invisible to everyone including the person doing it.

## Short angle
—

## Notable quote
> The staying quiet when every part of me wants to step in and fix it.

## Raw caption
There's a version of parenting nobody talks about. The part where you're actively preparing someone to not need you.

To walk into rooms you've never been in. To handle things you won't be there for. To know her own worth so completely that she never waits for someone else to confirm it.

That's the goal I'm working toward every day. Even when it looks like nothing. The floor time. The letting her figure it out herself. The staying quiet when every part of me wants to step in and fix it.

I'm not raising her for the life I have. I'm raising her for the life she's going to build.

And I want her to build something I could never have imagined for her.

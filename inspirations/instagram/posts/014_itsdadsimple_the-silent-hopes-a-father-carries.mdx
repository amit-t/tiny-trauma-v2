# the silent hopes a father carries

## Friction
You hold a hundred hopes for a child and have to keep every one of them silent, because spoken they'd become a weight on her instead of you.

## Seed line
They're yours to carry, not hers to fulfill. The hopes that live in the back of every decision and never make it to your mouth.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the quiet arithmetic of parenthood, where the loudest love is the one you deliberately never say out loud.

## Short angle
—

## Notable quote
> They're yours to carry, not hers to fulfill.

## Raw caption
Here's what nobody tells you about being a dad to a daughter.

You carry silent hopes you'll never speak out loud. Not because they don't matter. Because you know they're yours to carry, not hers to fulfill.

I watch Avery every day and I hope things. Quietly. Constantly.

I hope she grows up kind but not so kind that people take advantage of her. Strong but not so tough that she forgets it's okay to fall apart sometimes. Confident enough to speak her mind but wise enough to know when to listen. Brave enough to take risks but grounded enough to know her limits.

I hope she loves fiercely and guards her heart when she needs to. I hope she's independent but never feels like she has to carry everything alone. I hope she knows her worth so deeply that settling never feels like an option.

These aren't expectations. They're just the quiet weight I carry as her papa.

Because daughters don't become who their fathers lecture them to be. They become who their fathers show them it's possible to be.

Hope quietly. Guide gently. Love unconditionally.

# the shade moves away when you sit down

## Friction
The crowd around you was attendance at your usefulness, and it takes one tired day to read the room correctly.

## Seed line
The moment he stopped to rest, even the shade that had comforted him seemed to move away. Stability is the first thing to leave when strength does.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the difference between people who stood with you and people who stood in your shade, and how only fatigue can tell them apart.

## Short angle
—

## Notable quote
> The moment he grew tired and paused for rest, even the shade that once comforted him seemed to move away.

## Raw caption
In these lines, the poet realizes the true nature of time and people through moments of weakness. As long as he was strong and offering support, he was surrounded by many, and his presence seemed valued. But the moment he grew tired and paused for rest, even the shade that once comforted him seemed to move away, symbolizing how stability itself feels uncertain when strength fades. When he stood firm, he was part of a crowd, important and needed—but when he fell, the same people quietly chose different paths. The poetry reveals a painful truth: time changes everything, and many relationships exist only as long as you remain strong.

Poet: Farhat Abbas Shah, renowned Pakistani Urdu poet (b. 15 November 1964, Jhang, Punjab).

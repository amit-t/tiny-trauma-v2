# you describe the pain the way you bore it

## Friction
Two people carry the same loss and tell two incompatible stories, and both are honest, because the telling is shaped by the carrying.

## Seed line
Whoever endures it a certain way will describe it that way. There is no neutral account of pain, only the shape your particular spine gave it.

## Voice
text-on-screen

## Essay angle
On why no two people's grief sounds alike even about the same thing, and why arguing about whose version is true misses the point.

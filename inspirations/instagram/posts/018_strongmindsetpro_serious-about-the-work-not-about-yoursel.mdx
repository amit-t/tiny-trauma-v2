# serious about the work, not about yourself

## Friction
Somewhere you learned that being taken seriously meant never being caught laughing, and you've been auditing your own face ever since.

## Seed line
Locked in, then ten minutes later laughing until your eyes water. The rare ones never decided seriousness and joy were a zero-sum trade.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the myth that gravity equals depth, and the people who quietly disprove it at the next desk.

## Short angle
—

## Notable quote
> Takes their work seriously but doesn't take themselves too seriously.

## Raw caption
You know what kind of person I find most magnetic?

The person who takes their work seriously but doesn't take themselves too seriously. The one who shows up fully locked in and then ten minutes later is laughing so hard their eyes water. That person is rare. Because somewhere along the way people got the idea that to be successful you have to be hard. Cold. Serious all the time. Like joy is somehow a distraction from greatness. But that's completely backwards. You can be driven AND fun. Hungry AND humble. Focused AND kind.

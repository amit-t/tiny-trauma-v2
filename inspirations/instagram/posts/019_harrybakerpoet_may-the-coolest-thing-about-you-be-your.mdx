# may the coolest thing about you be your warmth

## Friction
You spent years cultivating an interesting exterior and the kindest thing anyone could wish for you is that none of it sticks.

## Seed line
May the coolest thing about you be your warmth. A blessing that quietly disqualifies everything you've been performing as cool.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the long detour from wanting to be impressive to hoping to be kind, and how late the second wish arrives.

—

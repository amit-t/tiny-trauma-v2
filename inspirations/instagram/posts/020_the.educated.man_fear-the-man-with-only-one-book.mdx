# fear the man with only one book

## Friction
The danger was never the wide reader who can hold ten ideas; it's the one who has one and has confused having it with reading it.

## Seed line
Fear the man who has only one book, considers it sacred, and has never actually read it. Certainty is cheapest where curiosity is absent.

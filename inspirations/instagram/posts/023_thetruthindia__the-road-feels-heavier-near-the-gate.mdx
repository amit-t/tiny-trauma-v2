# the road feels heavier near the gate

## Friction
The last stretch before the thing you wanted is the heaviest, and nobody warns you that arrival has a tax collected in dread.

## Seed line
The journey often feels heavier right before the gates of the destination. The closer you get, the more there is to lose by not arriving.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On the specific dread of almost-there, and why fear scales with proximity, not distance.

—

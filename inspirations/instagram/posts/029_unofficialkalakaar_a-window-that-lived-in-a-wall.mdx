# a window that lived in a wall

## Friction
Some art doesn't perform at you, it sits down across from you and waits, and you don't notice it changed something until later.

## Seed line
A window lived in a wall. Vinod Kumar Shukla's title, and the whole of his magical realism: the impossible kept this domestic, this quiet.

## Voice
observed

## Essay angle
On art that holds a conversation instead of a pitch, and grieving a writer (d. December 2025) by finally reading the title you'd only heard.

## Short angle
—

## Notable quote
> A window lived in a wall. (Deewar Mein Ek Khirkee Rehti Thi — Vinod Kumar Shukla)

## Raw caption
Chaar Phool Hain aur Duniya Hai by Achal Mishra that you can watch on YouTube.
Every film by director Achal Mishra has changed something inside me. This movie talks to you. Specially, if you are an artist. Very few films can actually have a conversation with the audience. For this film, Achal Mishra, his team and Manav Kaul spent two afternoons with Hindi writer Vinod Kumar Shukla & his family at their Raipur home. Vinod Kumar Shukla (1 January 1937 – 23 December 2025) was an Indian Hindi writer known for his writing style that often bordered on magical realism. His works included the novels Naukar ki Kameez (Servant's shirt) and Deewar Mein Ek Khirkee Rehti Thi (A window lived in a wall). The latter won the Sahitya Akademi Award for the best Hindi work in 1999.

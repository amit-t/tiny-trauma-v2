It is not the age. The age, by now, is not interesting. The age is a small, dignified number that I do not, particularly, mind.

It is the _obligation_. The obligation to want a thing — a meal, a gift, a small ceremony — and to name the thing in advance, in the right tone, so that the people who love me have something to actually do.

It is the further obligation to _receive_ the thing, on the day, in front of the people who got it for you, and to feel the correct amount about it, audibly, on cue.

I am bad at all three steps. I have always been bad at all three steps. I have, over the years, refined a small, polite version of _cagey_ that gets me through the day with my dignity intact and the people who love me, mostly, unbruised.

It is a small failure of generosity on my part. They are trying to love me, on the day, in the language that is most legible to them — a meal, a gift, a small ceremony — and the least I can do is be a good audience for the love.

[[aside]] ↳ I am working on it. it is slow.
